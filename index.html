📖 Detailed Step-by-Step User Guide

Step 1: Draw or Type Your Signature on the Ink Pad · [Signature Pad Canvas (#pad)]Draw your signature smoothly on the `#pad` canvas using a mouse, finger, or stylus (or switch to the **Type** tab to generate a calligraphic signature from your typed name).✓ Result: High-resolution transparent ink strokes are captured locally.
Step 2: Lock Signature & Drop Your PDF Document · [Use Signature (#useSig) & PDF Dropzone (#dz)]Click **Use this signature (`#useSig`)**, then drop the `.pdf` file you need to sign onto `#dz`. Select the target page number from the page dropdown (`#pageSel`).✓ Result: The selected PDF page renders live on the preview canvas (`#cv`).
Step 3: Enable Date Stamp & Click Page to Place Signature · [Stamp Date (#addDate) & Page Canvas (#cv)]Check **Stamp today's date (`#addDate`)** and adjust the signature size slider if needed. Click directly on the signature line on the PDF preview canvas (`#cv`) to position your ink.✓ Result: Your signature and date stamp appear at the exact coordinates you clicked.
Step 4: Flatten & Download Signed PDF · [Flatten & Download Button (#stamp)]Click **Flatten & download signed PDF (`#stamp`)**. `pdf-lib` embeds the signature XObject and date text directly into the page content stream.✓ Result: Signed, flattened `.pdf` saved to your machine—nothing ever left your browser.

🎛️ UI Controls & Shortcuts Reference

  • Signature Pad (`#pad`) & Clear (`#clearPad`) — Pressure-aware pointer canvas supporting mouse, touch, and pen/stylus input.
  • Use this signature (`#useSig`) — Captures the drawn ink as a transparent PNG ready for placement on any PDF page.
  • Page Selector (`#pageSel`) & Stamp Date (`#addDate`) — Switch between pages of a multi-page PDF and optionally append the current date below the signature.
  • Flatten & download (`#stamp`) — Bakes the signature and date into the PDF page content stream and downloads the signed PDF.

💡 Pro Tips & Real-World Workflows

  • On a laptop trackpad, draw slightly larger across the full width of `#pad`, then use the scale control to size it down neatly onto narrow signature lines.
  • Need to sign on multiple pages (e.g., initialing Page 1 and signing Page 4)? Place and stamp Page 1, then drop the resulting PDF to sign Page 4.
  • Because the ink is flattened into the page stream rather than added as a floating annotation, recipients cannot accidentally drag or delete your signature.
